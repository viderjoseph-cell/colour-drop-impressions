About us

A decade of turning ideas into ink, fabric and signage.

Founded in 2013, Colour Drop Impression has grown from a small decorating-and-printing outfit into one of Accra's trusted design and print studios. Our expertise spans designing, printing, packaging and rebranding — and over ten years and hundreds of projects, we've built a team of specialists dedicated to getting every detail right.

We serve everyday individuals ordering custom shirts and personal keepsakes, right alongside local businesses, corporate clients, churches and schools that need reliable marketing materials. Whatever the brief, our mission stays the same: bring your ideas to life, with a high-quality, durable finish every time.

  • Long-term relationships, not one-off jobs
  • Quick to respond as your needs change
  • Complete customer satisfaction, every order
  • Clear, professional communication throughout

Maame Yaa Dufie

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Maame holds an MSc in Logistics & Supply Chain Management from Coventry University (UK) and a BA in Publishing Studies from KNUST, with a background in project management and community mobilisation. She also leads CDI Solutions.
